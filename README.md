# Alireza Ghaderi, PhD

**Aerospace engineer | AI researcher | University instructor | Autonomous systems builder**

I work across civil aviation, artificial intelligence, robotics, flight
simulation, and autonomous systems. My projects connect research with practical
engineering: pilot-state monitoring, realistic flight simulation, visual
autonomy, multi-vehicle operations, and field-ready developer tools.

- PhD in Aerospace Engineering, Sharif University of Technology
- Flight Test Laboratory Instructor
- CPL/IR pilot with ATP Integrated theoretical ground training completed
- Open to international research, technical leadership, advisory, and
  commercial collaboration

## Selected Systems

| Project | Engineering scope |
| --- | --- |
| [MDS - Mission-Directed Swarm](https://github.com/alireza787b/mavsdk_drone_show) | An open-source MAVLink fleet operations platform for PX4 drones, simulation, synchronized drone shows, search-and-rescue workflows, cooperative autonomy, and operational validation. |
| [PixEagle](https://github.com/alireza787b/PixEagle) | A modular computer-vision and autonomous tracking suite for PX4 drones and ground vehicles, combining OpenCV, YOLO, MAVSDK, streaming, telemetry, and a real-time web dashboard. |
| [PX4-XPlane](https://github.com/alireza787b/px4xplane) | A cross-platform X-Plane plugin connecting PX4 SITL with realistic aircraft simulation for fixed-wing, multirotor, hybrid VTOL, and advanced-air-mobility research. |
| [Python Gaze and Face Tracker](https://github.com/alireza787b/Python-Gaze-Face-Tracker) | A real-time eye, iris, facial-landmark, blink, head-pose, and gaze tracking toolkit for aviation research, human-computer interaction, and interactive systems. |
| [DroneSphere](https://github.com/alireza787b/dronesphere) | An MCP-based interface for structured natural-language interaction with PX4 simulation and drone workflows, combining deterministic safety checks with AI-agent orchestration. |
| [MAVLink Anywhere](https://github.com/alireza787b/mavlink-anywhere) | A portable MAVLink routing dashboard for companion computers, ground-control links, LTE, Wi-Fi, VPNs, UDP, serial connections, and field telemetry. |
